## 2. Language-by-Language Impact Re-Assessment

### 1. Konkani: Rejection Verdict Stands
- **Observed Metrics in Patch v2**:
  - Long slice middle recall dropped: $40.0\% \to 24.0\%$ ($-16.0\%$).
  - Long slice overall F1 dropped: $34.82\% \to 30.40\%$ ($-4.42\%$).
  - Standard Gold-Dev F1 dropped: $96.69\% \to 92.31\%$ ($-4.38\%$).
- **Re-Assessment**:
  The patched model failed to generalize even when tested against the rule tagger's own injected ORGs, while simultaneously degrading on standard dev.
- **Verdict**: Rejection of `patched_v2_konkani.pt` in favor of baseline `selftrained_konkani_best.pt` **STANDS**.

### 2. Sinhala: Rejection Verdict Stands
- **Observed Metrics in Patch v2**:
  - Long slice middle recall: $15.38\% \to 15.38\%$ ($+0.0\%$).
  - Long slice overall F1: $20.18\% \to 20.63\%$ ($+0.45\%$).
  - Standard Gold-Dev F1 dropped: $99.24\% \to 96.65\%$ ($-2.59\%$, with 6 false-positive ORGs).
- **Re-Assessment**:
  The patched model introduced noise and false positives on standard dev without producing any meaningful gain on long list sentences.
- **Verdict**: Rejection of `patched_v2_sinhala.pt` in favor of baseline `augmented_sinhala.pt` **STANDS**.

### 3. Assamese: Advance Verdict is COMPROMISED
- **Observed Metrics in Patch v2**:
  - Long slice middle recall: $46.51\% \to 51.16\%$ ($+4.65\%$, $+2$ entities out of 43).
  - Long slice ORG F1: $2.94\% \to 11.43\%$ ($+8.49\%$).
  - Long slice overall F1: $57.83\% \to 61.90\%$ ($+4.07\%$).
  - Standard Gold-Dev F1: $98.92\% \to 98.22\%$ ($-0.70\%$).
  - Standard Gold-Dev Long F1: $100.0\% \to 100.0\%$ ($+0.00\%$).
  - Standard Gold-Dev ORG F1: $96.55\% \to 96.55\%$ ($+0.00\%$).
- **Critical Re-Assessment**:
  1. The apparent $+8.49\%$ ORG F1 gain was measured on a file where **64 of the 65 ORGs were injected by `DedicatedORGRuleTagger`**.
  2. Because the training pipeline used synthetic entity replacement and weak-supervision data generated by the same rule set, the model simply memorized the rule-tagger's patterns.
  3. On the genuinely independent, standard Gold-Dev set, the model showed **zero gain** in the long bucket ($+0.00\%$) and zero gain on ORG ($+0.00\%$), while regressing overall by $-0.70\%$.
  4. The only genuine improvement is 2 additional middle entities (22 vs 20) in long sentences.
- **Verdict**: Advancing `patched_v2_assamese.pt` over `augmented_assamese.pt` cannot be justified on the basis of `long_multientity_eval.conll`. It should be treated as **UNVERIFIED / DISPUTED**.
